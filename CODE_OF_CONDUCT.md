# Code of Conduct

OffPDF welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels,
and points of view. Keep project spaces useful, respectful, and focused on the
work.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of technical experience.
- Help keep issues and pull requests clear, reproducible, and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish
someone else's private information or confidential documents.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository and in other spaces where someone is
representing OffPDF. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to **kul3562@gmail.com**. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical.
